Dr. Fethi Chebil

International Aviation & Infrastructure Executive

CEO & Founder of VPorts.com & AEPM International | Advisor to PPMDC CEO | Advanced Air Mobility & Private Aviation Leader

Executive Summary

With over two decades of experience in the aviation and infrastructure sectors, Dr. Fethi Chebil has successfully led transformative projects shaping the future of global transportation. His expertise spans Advanced Air Mobility (AAM), private aviation, airport infrastructure, and the development of specialized services for high-value and mission-critical passenger groups.

Current Leadership

Advisor to CEO, PPMDC

Saudi Binladin Group Subsidiary

As an Advisor to the CEO of PPMDC, a fully owned subsidiary of the Saudi Binladin Group (SBG), he supports the operation of a dedicated terminal for pilgrims at Jeddah Airport—one of the most critical passenger hubs in Saudi Arabia. Alongside a dedicated team, he is expanding PPMDC's global business footprint by developing new concessions and strengthening international partnerships.

CEO & Founder, AEPM International

Leading the development of Commercial Important Passenger (CIP) lounges worldwide, including a landmark 50-year concession at GRU Airport in Brazil (divested in 2024).

CEO & Founder, VPorts.com

Driving global efforts to integrate sustainable Advanced Air Mobility into everyday transportation systems.

How It Works: Two-Step Review Process

1

Submit Your Proposal

  • Subject and key messages
  • Why this benefits readers
  • Content type (case study, point of view, lesson learned, etc.)

2

Expert Committee Review

Your proposal is reviewed by a committee of three aviation infrastructure experts. If approved, you'll be invited to submit the full article.

Committee reviews the full article for:

  • Quality and relevance
  • Consistency with reader expectations
  • Factual accuracy and insights
  • Request for changes or direct publishing

Quality Control: This two-step process ensures that Flight Deck maintains the highest standards. We're not looking for marketing content or vendor pitches—we want real expertise from practitioners who've been in the field.
